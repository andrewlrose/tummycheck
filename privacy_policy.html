Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

TummyCheck: FODMAP Scanner ("TummyCheck", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Rose Apps, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles device access, data, and permissions.

Privacy-First Architecture: TummyCheck does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information. We operate on a local-first architecture: your dietary sensitivity profile, scan history, Safe List favorites, store selections, and captured product photos remain securely on your personal device.

1. Device Permissions and Data Handling

TummyCheck requests access to specific device capabilities solely to deliver core app functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used to scan product barcodes in real time and recognize ingredient text on food packaging and restaurant menus using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). Live camera frames for barcode recognition and menu analysis are processed strictly in-memory and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Product Photos (On-Device Storage & Optional Cloud Backup): When you scan a product or choose to capture/retake a photo, a small thumbnail image (~128px JPEG) is saved locally on your device to help you visually recognize products in your History and Safe List.

    Backup Behavior: By default, product photos are stored in your device's private non-backed-up storage (noBackupFilesDir) and are never included in Google Auto Backup or uploaded to the cloud. If you explicitly enable "Include photos in Google backup" in Profile & Settings, the photo database is stored in app files storage so it can be restored if you switch devices. Photos are never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13 (API 33) and higher, the app requests notification permission only if you choose to enable the Safe List Freshness reminder in Settings. This reminder performs a local check on your device and alerts you if a saved product has not been reviewed in 30 days (since manufacturers occasionally update ingredient formulations). It does not send marketing notifications.
  • Local Storage (SQLite & SharedPreferences): The app stores your scan history, custom sensitivity profile toggles (fructans, GOS, lactose, excess fructose, polyols, ambiguous seasonings), Safe List favorites with aisle categories, My Stores supermarket selections, UI theme preferences, and free scan quota counters exclusively in your device's private storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Network Communication

TummyCheck initiates network connections only for the following specific purposes:

  • Open Food Facts API: When you scan a barcode not already cached in your local database, the app queries the public Open Food Facts database using the numeric barcode (GTIN) to retrieve the product name, brand, and ingredient list. No user identifiers, device IDs, advertising IDs, IP tracking tokens, or location coordinates are sent with this request. Food product data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases for TummyCheck Pro are handled directly by the official Google Play Billing Library. Payment credentials, credit cards, billing addresses, and transaction security are processed entirely by Google. The app receives only a cryptographically signed purchase token to unlock Pro features. The billing entitlement cache is explicitly excluded from cloud backups to prevent invalid state transfer.

3. User-Initiated Data Export & Sharing

The app allows you to export your saved Safe List, scan history, or learned barcode library as plain text or CSV files via the standard Android system share sheet. This export occurs exclusively when you explicitly tap an export or share button. You control which external application (such as email, messaging, or cloud drive) receives your exported data. TummyCheck never transmits your exported files automatically or silently.

4. Multilingual Restaurant Scripts ("Show Your Server")

When using the Dining / Restaurant OCR mode, the app generates polite dietary cards in English, Spanish, French, and Italian to help communicate dietary preferences to restaurant staff. All translation templates and script generation logic are bundled locally within the app and operate entirely offline with zero network transmission.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

TummyCheck contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking pixels, and zero behavioral telemetry SDKs. We do not track you across applications or websites, and we never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or dietary information.

6. Children's Privacy

TummyCheck is designed for general audiences aged 18 and older who are managing dietary preferences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal identity information, no personal data from minors is processed or stored on our servers.

7. Informational Nutrition Disclaimer

TummyCheck is an informational nutritional reference tool based on published low-FODMAP dietary literature. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, clinical service, or substitute for professional medical or dietary consultation. Always consult a qualified physician, gastroenterologist, or registered dietitian before beginning or altering an elimination diet.
